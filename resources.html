Government of India

Central credit schemes for MSMEs

Credit guarantees and subsidies that make bank and NBFC loans easier to get. You apply through a lender. The scheme supports the loan, not the borrower directly.

Mutual Credit Guarantee Scheme (MCGS-MSME)

NCGTC

A 60% guarantee to lenders for loans that finance plant, machinery and equipment.

Loan size
Up to ₹100 crore guaranteed per borrower. The project can be larger.
Use
Equipment or machinery must be at least 75% of project cost
Tenor
Up to 8 years, including a 2-year principal moratorium, for loans up to ₹50 crore
Eligibility
MSMEs with Udyam registration

Most relevant to mid-sized manufacturers expanding capacity. Launched January 2025 and revised in March 2026. ncgtc.in

Credit Guarantee Fund Trust for Micro and Small Enterprises

CGTMSE

Guarantees collateral-free loans to micro and small enterprises.

Loan size
Up to ₹10 crore per borrower for guarantees from 1 April 2025, up from ₹5 crore
Cover
Typically 75–90% of the amount in default, depending on category and loan size
Cost
Annual guarantee fee, usually passed on to the borrower
Eligibility
Micro and small enterprises; term loans and working capital

For loans without collateral or third-party guarantees. cgtmse.in

Pradhan Mantri Mudra Yojana

MUDRA

Collateral-free loans for non-farm micro enterprises, in four tiers.

Shishu
Up to ₹50,000
Kishore
₹50,000 to ₹5 lakh
Tarun
₹5 lakh to ₹10 lakh
Tarun Plus
₹10 lakh to ₹20 lakh, for borrowers who have repaid a Tarun loan

Through banks, NBFCs and microfinance institutions. mudra.org.in

Prime Minister's Employment Generation Programme

PMEGP · KVIC

A credit-linked subsidy for setting up new micro enterprises.

Project size
Up to ₹50 lakh (manufacturing) or ₹20 lakh (services)
Subsidy
Margin money of 15–35% of project cost, by category and location
Eligibility
New units only; individuals and certain groups

Apply online through the KVIC portal. kviconline.gov.in

Stand-Up India

SIDBI

Bank loans for greenfield enterprises promoted by women or SC/ST entrepreneurs.

Loan size
₹10 lakh to ₹1 crore, composite term loan and working capital
Use
Greenfield projects in manufacturing, services, trading or allied agriculture

Lenders include scheduled commercial banks. standupmitra.in

PM Vishwakarma

Ministry of MSME

Concessional, collateral-free credit with skills and toolkit support for traditional artisans and craftspeople.

Loan size
₹1 lakh, then ₹2 lakh in a second tranche
Rate
5% concessional interest to the borrower

pmvishwakarma.gov.in

TReDS invoice discounting

RBI-regulated

Online platforms where MSMEs sell invoices raised on large corporates, PSUs and government buyers to banks and NBFCs, often at competitive rates.

Use
Working capital against receivables, without recourse to the MSME
Platforms
Several RBI-authorised platforms operate

Useful when large buyers pay on long credit terms.

Jan Samarth portal

Government of India

A single portal to check eligibility for, and apply to, several credit-linked government schemes, including Mudra, PMEGP and Stand-Up India.

Use
Eligibility checks and digital applications

jansamarth.in

Summaries as of 23 November 2026. Scheme terms change through government notifications. Confirm current guidelines on the official site or with your lender before applying. AK Advisors is not affiliated with any of these schemes.

MSME classification

Is your business an MSME?

A business must stay within both limits. The thresholds were revised from 1 April 2025, so many mid-sized companies now qualify as medium enterprises.

MSME classification by investment and turnover
CategoryInvestment in plant, machinery or equipmentAnnual turnover
MicroUp to ₹2.5 croreUp to ₹10 crore
SmallUp to ₹25 croreUp to ₹100 crore
MediumUp to ₹125 croreUp to ₹500 crore

Register on the Udyam portal to access MSME schemes. Source: Ministry of MSME, msme.gov.in.

State governments

State incentives for MSME borrowing

Most states add capital or interest subsidies on top of central schemes through their industrial or MSME policies. Benefits depend on location, sector and investment, and usually need prior registration with the state.

Selected state MSME credit incentives
StateWhat is typically availableWhere to check
GujaratInterest subsidy on term loans for eligible MSMEs under the state industrial policy, plus capital subsidies and other incentivesIndustries Commissionerate
MaharashtraPackage Scheme of Incentives, with higher support in less developed regions such as Vidarbha and Marathwada. CMEGP adds to PMEGP for new micro unitsDirectorate of Industries
Tamil NaduNEEDS: capital subsidy and interest subvention for first-generation entrepreneurs. MSME policy subsidies by districtMSME Department portal
Uttar PradeshMSME Promotion Policy: capital and interest subsidies for eligible units, with higher rates in less developed regionsDepartment of MSME

Indicative only. State policies are revised regularly and rates and caps vary by scheme. Confirm current terms with the state industries department before relying on them.

Not sure which schemes apply? We look at central and state support when we structure a financing case, so the lender pack reflects any guarantee or subsidy you are eligible for.
