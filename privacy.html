Privacy notice

How we handle the details you share with us through this website.

Last updated 27 November 2026

Who we are

AK Advisors and Consultants arranges debt on behalf of borrowers. For any question about your information, write to queries@akadvisors.in or call +91 98715 88733.

What we collect

When you request a callback, we receive the details you enter: your name, company, mobile number, email address (optional), loan requirement, indicative amount, timing, any details you add and your preferred time to call. We do not ask for financial statements, identity documents or banking details through this website.

How we use it

  • To contact you about your enquiry and arrange a confidential discussion.
  • If you then engage us, to carry out the mandate you agree with us.

We do not sell your information or use it for unrelated marketing. We share it with a lender only with your approval, as part of a mandate.

Service providers

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Callback requests are delivered to our inbox by Web3Forms. Fonts and website code libraries load from Google Fonts, cdnjs and jsDelivr. These providers process technical data such as your IP address so the site can work. If you choose to send your request by WhatsApp or email instead, those services handle the message under their own terms.

Cookies and storage

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. If we switch on website analytics, we use GoatCounter, which counts visits and button clicks without cookies and without identifying you. The document checklist on the Resources page remembers your ticks in your own browser only, and you can clear them at any time.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for as long as needed to respond and, if you engage us, for the period required for the mandate and our legal and professional records. We then delete them.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the information you sent, or withdraw your consent to be contacted, by writing to queries@akadvisors.in. We aim to handle personal data in line with India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.
