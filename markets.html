Policy and lending rates

Where rates stand today

The RBI raised the repo rate by 25 bps on 7 October 2026 and moved its stance to calibrated tightening. Banks have since been raising lending benchmarks.

External benchmark rates (SBI)

Most new MSME and retail floating-rate loans are linked to an external benchmark, usually the repo rate.

CRP is the credit risk premium and BSP the business strategy premium. Both are set by the lender for each borrower.

Repo rate

The rate at which the RBI lends to banks overnight. Changes flow into external-benchmark loans at their next reset, usually within three months.

MCLR

Each bank's internal benchmark, based on its marginal cost of funds. A loan priced at MCLR plus a spread resets on the date set in the sanction, often every 6 or 12 months.

What it means for you

Check your benchmark and reset dates. After a policy rate rise, a borrower on a 1-year MCLR reset may not see the change for months, while a repo-linked loan moves sooner.

    India and the world

    How India compares

    India saves a large share of its income but lends less to the private sector, relative to GDP, than most large economies. That gap explains why bank credit for mid-sized companies is still relatively scarce.
